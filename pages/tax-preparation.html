Prepare for your visit

A short checklist to get started.

Your exact documents depend on your situation. This list is a useful starting point; we’ll confirm what applies when you book.

Identity & household

  • Photo ID for taxpayer and spouse
  • Social Security or ITIN letters for household members on the return
  • Last year’s return, if available

Income

  • W-2 and 1099 forms
  • SSA, retirement, or unemployment statements
  • Gig, self-employment, rental, or cash income summaries

Expenses & credits

  • Childcare, education, mortgage, property tax, charitable, and medical records
  • Business mileage, supplies, software, phone, and home-office records
  • Health coverage forms, if applicable

Other helpful records

  • Bank routing and account numbers for direct deposit or payment
  • IRS or state tax letters
  • Receipts or summaries related to questions you want to discuss

Questions

Tax preparation FAQs.

Can I book before all of my tax forms arrive?

Yes. You can reserve an appointment early. Bring the documents you have and tell us which forms are still expected.

Can I meet after work or on the weekend?

Yes. Evening and weekend appointments are available. Ask about remote or in-person options when you schedule.

Can you help with a prior-year return or an amendment?

We can discuss the documents and filing steps involved. Contact us first so we can confirm whether the work fits our current services.

What if I received an IRS or state notice?

Bring the full notice and the tax year it concerns. We can help you understand what information is requested and organize records for the next step. We do not provide representation before tax agencies.

Scope: Tax notice support is limited to the preparation and administrative assistance agreed for your appointment. FlexPoint Services does not provide representation before the IRS or state tax agencies.
